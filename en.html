Meet Mobina Shahri

Beauty Professional, Educator, and Trainer with International Experience

Mobina Shahri is an experienced beauty professional and educator with more than 18 years of industry experience in nail, hair, beauty, and skin care. Her work combines advanced technical expertise, structured instruction, and years of professional practice.

Over the years, she has trained more than 800 students in Iran, represented leading beauty brands in international competitions, and built a reputation as a national instructor. Today, she continues her work in Düsseldorf through private treatments and specialized beauty training for Persian-speaking residents in Europe.
